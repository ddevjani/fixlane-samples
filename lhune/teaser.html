The 5 that matter most

What a shopper runs into, measured on your store.

The same rule picks these for every store: how much each one matters, how many shoppers it reaches and how sure we are. Each shows what we saw and why it costs sales. The fix for your theme is in the full report.

CriticalTracking and ads

1. Your product pages drop the codes that link ad clicks to sales

What we saw

We opened /products/amara-bare-brilliant-round-diamond-tennis-bracelet-white-gold with a test ad link. In our test, the page rewrote its own address and dropped the ad tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, gclid, fbclid and ttclid), and the Google Ads and Meta tags could not store the click code they use to credit later sales to your ads.

Why it costs sales

Google says the click code must reach the final landing page for conversions to be tracked. Without it, campaigns can look like they do not pay, and bidding learns from fewer sales.

Source: Google Ads Help, about auto-tagging, read Oct 23, 2026

In the full report

The line in your theme that rewrites the address, the fix, and a test link you can open to check it yourself.

HighMobile

2. On a phone, shoppers have to scroll to find Add to Cart

What we saw

On 3 of 3 product pages we checked on an iPhone, Add to Cart sits below the first screen, most of a screen down, and we saw no buy button follow the shopper down the page.

Why it costs sales

On the median Shopify store, 5.5% of phone visits add anything to the cart. The buy button is where that happens, so it has to be easy to reach.

Source: Littledata Shopify benchmarks, data Jul 20 to Oct 17, 2026

In the full report

The change that puts the price and Add to Cart where a phone shopper can reach them, written for your theme, with a re-check.

On an iPhone: the Amara Bare Round Tennis Bracelet page.

MediumProduct pages

3. Your product pages give a making or shipping time but no arrival date

What we saw

On 3 product pages we checked, the buy box gives a making or shipping time, but we saw no arrival date near Add to Cart.

Why it costs sales

Baymard's testing found shoppers stall when they see only a shipping speed and try to work out the arrival day. Its benchmark found 41% of sites show no date among their delivery options at checkout, and Baymard advises showing the date on the product page too.

Source: Baymard Institute, shipping speed vs delivery date, cited in our catalog Oct 21, 2026

In the full report

An arrival date that follows your real making and shipping times, placed by the button, with the setup for your theme.

97 of the 171 products we checked have fewer than 4 photos or videos, including 7 best sellers. The typical product has 3.

Why it costs sales

Baymard found 56% of shoppers start a product page by looking at the photos.

Source: Baymard Institute, image resolution and zoom, cited in our catalog Oct 21, 2026

MediumCart and checkout

5. Your product pages have no quick-pay button

What we saw

On 3 of 3 product pages we checked on an iPhone, we saw Add to Cart but no quick-pay button such as Buy with Shop Pay.

Why it costs sales

A quick-pay button lets a shopper buying one item skip the cart. One store's test found more sales after adding it; another store's test found more sales after removing it, a less certain result.

Sources: Blend Commerce, adding express checkout to the product page, read Oct 23, 2026; Blend Commerce, removing express checkout from the product page, read Oct 23, 2026
